North and east from the New York suburbs, through country that has been telling
these stories since before the country existed.

## The Hudson Valley

**Spook Rock Road** first, then **Pine Bush**, which spent the 1980s and 90s as
one of the most reported UFO hotspots in the eastern United States and has the
museum to show for it.

**Headless Horseman** near Esopus is a large seasonal operation rather than a
roadside stop, running autumn only. Washington Irving set the original story
about fifty miles south, and the whole valley has been trading on it ever since.

**The Kaatskill Kaleidoscope** at Mount Tremper is built inside a converted grain
silo and is, by a considerable margin, the largest in the world. It is a proper
attraction with a ticket.

## Adirondacks and Vermont

**Rt 30 Alien** near Lake Pleasant is a quick photograph on a long drive.

**Burlington** has the world's tallest filing cabinet — thirty-eight drawers
stacked by an artist as a protest sculpture, which is a better reason than most.

## Into Maine

**Rumford** has Paul Bunyan and Babe, which the Midwest would dispute but Maine
claims regardless. Then a long run up the coast to **Addison** and the world's
largest lobster trap.

## Practical

Four days, and the last leg to Addison is further than it looks — Maine's coast
road is slow by design.

Autumn is the season for this route. It is also when the haunted attractions run,
which is either convenient or crowded depending on your view.
