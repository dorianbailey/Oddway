Samantha Murphy is the artist behind SMMorbidCreations, based in Meyersdale,
Pennsylvania. Working across acrylic painting, sculpture, and tattoo art,
Samantha creates work rooted in the dark, unusual, and haunting sides of life.

Drawing inspiration from both the natural world and the macabre, her work
explores the beauty that can be found in subjects many people tend to overlook
or turn away from. Through canvas, clay, and skin, she brings eerie and
unconventional ideas to life with a style that feels haunting, detailed, and
deeply personal.

Her work often blends organic forms with darker imagery, creating pieces that
feel both familiar and unsettling at the same time. Rather than avoiding the
strange or uncomfortable, Samantha leans into it, using art as a way to
highlight the beauty hidden within decay, darkness, and the unusual.

SMMorbidCreations is a home for the hauntingly beautiful, shining a light on
the darker corners of art and giving overlooked subjects a space to be seen in
a completely different way.
