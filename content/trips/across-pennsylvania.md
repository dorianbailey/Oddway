The direct route between Pittsburgh and Philadelphia is five hours of turnpike.
This is not that. It is roughly the same journey with the interesting parts left
in, and it takes two days because the good bits are on two-lane roads.

## The shape of it

You start with a hill in North Park that rolls the wrong way, drop south to
Kecksburg for the acorn behind the fire station, and pick up a second gravity
hill at New Paris — which is worth doing precisely because you have just done
one, and the effect is identical in a completely different landscape.

Then the long middle stretch to Centralia, which is the only stop on this route
that is not fun.

Philadelphia and Eastern State finish it.

## Centralia deserves a warning

It is a borough with residents, not a ruin. The Graffiti Highway was buried in
2020 and no longer exists. You can legally drive the current alignment of Route
61 and look from the road, and that is the whole of the legal access.

Go, but go understanding that five people live there and the ground vents carbon
monoxide.

## Where to break it

Somewhere around Bedford or Harrisburg, depending on how long you spend at the
gravity hills. Both have places to stay and neither is a detour.
