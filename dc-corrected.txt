Stops: 1–100

ACCESS KEY
open = generally accessible during normal public hours
limited = tickets, tours, scheduled access, changing display status, security screening, or restricted hours may apply
roadside = best viewed from lawful public sidewalk/road/trail space
view-only = exterior/site can be observed, but the relevant property or interior is not open for ordinary entry
seasonal = access depends on season or scheduled operation
private = do not enter without explicit permission

ODDWAY RESEARCH NOTE
Every entry has a routable location and a specific OddWay reason to stop. Coordinates are wayfinding pins, not permission to enter private, military, secured, cemetery-restricted, or otherwise controlled property. Paranormal material is described as lore rather than fact. Primary, government, museum, preservation, or official local sources are used where practical; discovery/competitor sources are retained only where a stronger first-party page was not available or did not document the oddity clearly.
