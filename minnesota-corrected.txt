OddWay — Minnesota Complete
120 Minnesota stops
Stops 1–120
Prepared September 10, 2026

Access key: open = normally accessible during posted/public hours; limited = seasonal, scheduled, ticketed, cemetery-hours, ferry/boat-dependent, appointment-only, or check-ahead access; roadside = view from a safe lawful public area; private = private property, do not enter without permission; seasonal = primarily accessible during a defined season; view-only = observe from a lawful public viewpoint and do not enter the feature.

Research note: This set prioritizes real, routable places with a specific OddWay reason to stop. First-party, government, museum, preservation, or official tourism sources are used wherever practical. Paranormal, UFO, cryptid, and folklore claims are described as reports or traditions rather than established fact. Sacred Indigenous and archaeological sites are presented as cultural landscapes that should be visited respectfully. Active industrial, underground, restricted, and structurally closed sites are labeled conservatively.
