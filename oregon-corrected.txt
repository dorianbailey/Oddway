OddWay — Oregon Complete
120 Oregon stops
Stops 1–120
Prepared September 10, 2026

Access key: open = normally accessible during posted/public hours; limited = seasonal, scheduled, ticketed, cemetery-hours, appointment-only, weather/road/tide-dependent, private-access-dependent, restoration-dependent, or check-ahead access; roadside = view from a safe lawful public area; private = private property, do not enter without permission; seasonal = primarily accessible during a defined season; view-only = observe from a lawful public viewpoint and do not enter restricted or private areas.

Research note: This set prioritizes places with a real, routable location and an OddWay-specific reason to stop. Paranormal, cryptid and supernatural claims are described as lore, reports or investigation subjects rather than fact. Active archaeological, sacred, cemetery, railroad, cave, mine, coastal, forest, state-park and private sites are labeled conservatively. First-party, government, museum, preservation and local-tourism sources are used wherever practical; discovery/competitor sources were used for research only when needed, with stronger primary sources listed whenever available. Coordinates are rounded to five decimal places and are intended as routable stop pins, not permission to cross private land, enter abandoned mines, leave maintained coastal viewpoints, or bypass closures.

2026 status note: Oregon Vortex is intentionally omitted because its current site says it remains closed after flood damage with reopening targeted for 2027. Tillamook Air Museum is also omitted because its official site says it is closed until further notice. Current temporary or seasonal limitations are called out in the entries below where they materially affect routing.
