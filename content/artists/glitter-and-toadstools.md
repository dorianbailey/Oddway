Glitter and Toadstools is the artist merch shop of Hannah Mathis, an
illustrator and maker whose work is inspired by folklore, fairytales,
cryptids, and urban legends. Mathis takes stories and creatures that are often
portrayed as dark or frightening and reimagines them through a more colorful,
whimsical, and playful lens.

Her work combines traditional and digital art techniques, with pieces created
using gouache, acrylic paint, pencil, ink, and digital illustration. This mix
of mediums gives her artwork a handmade quality while allowing her to
experiment with different styles, textures, and interpretations of familiar
legends.

Through Glitter and Toadstools, Hannah turns her original artwork into a wide
range of products, including original pieces, art prints, stickers, enamel
pins, card decks, hats, shirts, and other merchandise. Whether she is
illustrating a creature from folklore, putting a new spin on an old fairytale,
or creating something inspired by the stranger side of the world, her work
brings a sense of charm and curiosity to subjects that might otherwise feel
mysterious or unsettling.

For anyone drawn to folklore, cryptids, fairytales, or just slightly unusual
art, Glitter and Toadstools offers a fun and imaginative take on the weird and
wonderful.

And when it comes to cryptids, Hannah's favorite is Mothman — "basic, I know,"
as she puts it.
