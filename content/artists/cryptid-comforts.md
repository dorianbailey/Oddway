Cryptid Comforts is Lisa Russell's corner of the cryptid world, filled with
plush creatures, pet toys, original art, and assorted strange little things
built around folklore's weirdest residents.

Every design is hers.

The pet toys are probably where you stop first. Somebody had to answer the
question, "What should Mothman look like after a dog has been chewing on him
for twenty minutes?"

Lisa has answered it.

## The Roster

The obvious names are here. Mothman. The Flatwoods Monster. The Jersey Devil.
Chupacabra.

Then it gets more interesting.

There's a Loveland Frogman holding a tiny wand, a Thunderbird, Dogman, a bright
pink lake monster, and a squonk doing what squonks do best: sitting in the
corner crying.

Nothing on the table is pulled from a licensed character or somebody else's
artwork. Lisa draws every design herself, which means even the stranger corners
of cryptid lore get their own version.

## She Also Runs The Festivals

Lisa does more than make the creatures.

She cohosts Squonkapalooza in Johnstown, Pennsylvania, as well as Fearsome
Folklore Fest and UFO Day WV.

Squonkapalooza is already part of the OddWay event index.

There is a difference between setting up a table at a cryptid festival and
helping create the festival in the first place. Lisa does both, which probably
explains why Cryptid Comforts goes deeper than Mothman and Bigfoot.

She knows the whole roster.
