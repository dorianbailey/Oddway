OddWay — Washington Complete
124 Washington stops
Stops 1–124
Prepared September 10, 2026

Access key: open = normally accessible during posted/public hours; limited = seasonal, scheduled, ticketed, cemetery-hours, appointment-only, military/security-dependent, weather/road-dependent, private-access-dependent, or check-ahead access; roadside = view from a safe lawful public area; private = private property, do not enter without permission; seasonal = primarily accessible during a defined season; view-only = observe from a lawful public viewpoint and do not enter restricted infrastructure.

Research note: This set prioritizes places with a real, routable location and an OddWay-specific reason to stop. Paranormal claims are described as lore/reports rather than fact. Active military, archaeological, sacred, cemetery, railroad, border, national-park, and private sites are labeled conservatively. First-party, government, museum, preservation, or local-tourism sources are used wherever practical; competitor/discovery sources were used for research only when needed, with stronger primary sources listed whenever available. Coordinates are rounded to five decimal places and are intended as routable stop pins, not permission to cross private land or enter restricted areas.

Washington-specific note: Mount St. Helens, mountain-road, cave, and ghost-town access can change quickly with snow, wildfire, washouts, gates, and seasonal closures. Hanford, military, federal-campus, research-site, cemetery, school, and private-art locations require particular attention to posted access rules. The Manis Mastodon archaeological site itself is not routed here; the public museum exhibit is used instead.
